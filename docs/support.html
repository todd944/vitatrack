Frequently asked

Is my data backed up anywhere?
Not automatically — VitaTrack stores everything locally on your device only, with no account and no server. Use "Download my data" on the Profile tab to save a backup file periodically, and "Restore from backup" to load it back in.

Reminders aren't notifying me.
On iPhone, reminders need the app installed to your Home Screen (not just open in a browser tab) to send real notifications — see the Reminders section on the Profile tab for details.

I want to delete everything and start over.
Profile tab → Your data → Clear all data.

## Description (4000 chars max)

VitaTrack helps you keep track of the vitamins and supplements you take, without guessing whether
you're overdoing it or mixing something you shouldn't.

WHAT IT DOES
• Build a personal supplement schedule — pick days, dosages, and reminder times for anything in the
  built-in reference library or add your own custom product.
• Get a heads-up when a scheduled dose is near or above the upper limit for your age and sex, based
  on NIH dietary reference data.
• Check your supplement list against your medications for well-documented interaction concerns,
  using the NIH's RxNorm medication database.
• See a public FDA recall check for anything on your list.
• Track adherence with day-by-day history and streaks for everything you've scheduled.
• Optionally log mood and energy to notice your own patterns over time.
• Scan a barcode to find a product faster, or add anything by name.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
VitaTrack doesn't have accounts, logins, or a server. Everything — your schedule, history, and
health profile — stays stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. You can export
a backup file at any time, and delete everything with one tap.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
VitaTrack is a tracking and reference tool, not a substitute for medical advice. It doesn't diagnose
anything or tell you what to take. Reference data is adapted from NIH and FDA sources but isn't a
replacement for guidance from your doctor or pharmacist — always confirm decisions about supplements
and medications with them, especially before starting, stopping, or combining anything.

## App Privacy (Data Collection) — App Store Connect questionnaire
Given the app has no analytics, no backend, and no account system, the accurate answer to almost
every "Do you collect X" question is **No** — everything is stored locally via localStorage only.
This is worth stating plainly in the listing itself as a selling point, not just in the privacy
form.
